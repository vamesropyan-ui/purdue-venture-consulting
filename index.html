About Purdue Venture Consulting

Purdue Venture Consulting is a student organization that helps motivated students develop strategic thinking skills and connect with the startup ecosystem.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic excellence and real-world venture creation. We provide our members with unparalleled access to startup consulting opportunities, venture capital mentorship, and a network that extends far beyond campus.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a business major?

No. Our consultants come from engineering, business, computer science, and more. What matters is how you think.

What's the time commitment?

Plan for roughly 6–10 hours per week during an active project, including team meetings.

Do I need consulting experience?

Not at all. We train every member in the frameworks and skills you'll use on real client work.

What will I actually work on?

Live engagements for real startups — market research, competitive strategy, go-to-market, product, and more.
